# How to stay updated

This blog has an Atom/RSS feed here that you can use to stay updated: <https://conviviality-cooperatives.com/feed.xml>. Just copy that link into your reader.

Don't know what an RSS reader is? Well here's [a little guide](https://guides.library.yale.edu/keepingup/basics) and here's a piece from Cory Doctorow on why they are [they are so nice](https://doctorow.medium.com/you-should-be-using-an-rss-reader-76aed31151f9).

If you run into any troubles, reach out to <info@conviviality-cooperatives.com>
